Overview

A privileged corner of Kefalonia

“Ionian Treasure Suites” is in a beautiful location on Kefalonia island, in the village of Pessada.

The complex offers its guests five brand-new suites that accommodate two persons each. All are tastefully decorated and fully equipped with modern amenities: air-conditioning, a bedroom, a sitting area, a bathroom with shower, free Wi-Fi and a dining table in the kitchen.

“Ionian Treasure Suites” also features a swimming pool, a garden, a lovely view of the sea, parking and a private entrance.

The accommodation is in a privileged location — just 600 metres from beautiful Pessada Beach, and close to the village port with seasonal ferries to Zakynthos. Argostoli, Kefalonia's capital, is a ten-minute drive away, and guests have a choice of many sandy, organised beaches nearby.
