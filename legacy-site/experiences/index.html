Island guide

What to do with your days

Written by us, for guests staying here — the places we send people to, how far they are, and when they are at their best.

  • Beaches

    600 m

    The seven-minute walk to Pessada Beach

    Take the lane below the property and keep going downhill. A small pebble-and-sand cove with clear, sheltered water — best in the morning before the light goes hard.

  • Beaches

    15 min drive

    Lourdas and the long sandy bay

    Organised sand beneath Mount Ainos, with sunbeds, shade and a row of tavernas at the top of the road. The easiest full beach day with a car.

  • Beaches

    45 min drive

    Myrtos at golden hour

    The white-pebble bay everyone photographs. Go late in the afternoon: the crowds thin, the cliffs turn amber and the drive back over the pass is the point.

  • Village life

    10 min drive

    An evening in Argostoli

    Park near the Drapano bridge and walk the waterfront to Lithostroto for the evening volta, ice cream and the loggerhead turtles that drift by the fish quay.

  • Day trips

    1.2 km to the port

    Ferry across to Zakynthos

    Seasonal crossings leave from Pessada port just below us. Leave the car, walk on, and be on another island for lunch. We will check the current timetable for you.

  • Day trips

    45 min drive

    Mount Ainos and the black firs

    A national park of endemic Kefalonian fir, semi-wild horses and a view that takes in the whole island. Bring a layer — it is noticeably cooler at the top.

  • Food & wine

    25 min drive

    Robola wine on the Omala plain

    The island's crisp white grape, grown on limestone terraces. The cooperative near Valsamata pours tastings, and bottles travel home well.

  • Food & wine

    In your suite

    Market mornings, terrace dinners

    Every suite has a full kitchen and a grill. Buy fish and vegetables in Argostoli in the morning, then cook and eat outside as the light drops behind the hills.

When to come

Choosing your month

  • May – June

    24–29 °C, long light evenings

    Wildflowers still on the hillsides, warm sea, beaches almost to yourself. Our quietest good-weather months.

  • July – August

    30–34 °C, dependable sunshine

    Peak season: the Zakynthos ferry runs from Pessada port and every taverna is open. Book earliest for these dates.

  • September – early October

    24–30 °C, warmest sea of the year

    Our favourite window — grape harvest, softer light, and swimming that lasts well into October.
